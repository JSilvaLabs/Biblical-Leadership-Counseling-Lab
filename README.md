## 4 Interactive Studio Modules (`?tab=polity|corinthians|counseling|quiz`)
1. **Servant-Shepherd & Church Polity Lab (`?tab=polity` · `BML-101`, `BML-105`, `IML-205`)**
   - Explore **8 Canonical Leadership & Polity Archetypes** (*Exodus 18:13–27*, *Nehemiah 2–6*, *Ezekiel 34 & Psalm 23*, *Mark 10:42–45 & John 13*, *Acts 6:1–7*, *1 Timothy 3:1–7*, *1 Timothy 3:8–13*, *Titus 1:5–9 & 1 Peter 5:1–4*) with Hebrew/Greek office terms (`ro'eh`, `episkopos`, `presbyteros`, `diakonos`, `poimēn`), Berean Standard Bible (BSB) anchors, failure modes, and an interactive **1 Timothy 3 / 1 Peter 5 Shepherd Self-Audit**.
2. **Corinthian Pastoral Jurisprudence Lab (`?tab=corinthians` · `BML-103`)**
   - Step into **8 First-Century Corinthian Crisis Dockets** (*1 Cor 1–4 Factions*, *1 Cor 5 & 2 Cor 2 Redemptive Discipline*, *1 Cor 6 Believer Lawsuits*, *1 Cor 7 Marriage & Calling*, *1 Cor 8–10 Idol Meat & Conscience*, *1 Cor 11:17–34 Lord's Supper*, *1 Cor 12–14 Charismata & Order*, *2 Cor 4 & 12 Cruciform Weakness*) and deliberate between competing pastoral rulings to unlock Paul's apostolic verdict.
3. **Biblical Soul Care & Coaching Simulator (`?tab=counseling` · `BML-104`, `IML-203`, `AML-303`)**
   - Practice Christ-centered soul care across **8 Pastoral Care & Coaching Cases** (*Anxiety*, *Acute Grief*, *Bitterness*, *Ministry Burnout*, *Godly vs. Worldly Sorrow*, *Marital Communication*, *Habitual Sin*, *Vocational Discernment*), contrasting superficial moralism and secular reductionism with Scripture-anchored care, plus **1-Click `.md` Pastoral Care Plan Export**.
4. **15-Question Pastoral Leadership, Polity & Soul Care Exam (`?tab=quiz`)**
   - Comprehensive 15-question assessment across `BML 101–105` with instant theological rationales and live BDA Study Passport synchronization (`window.bdaReportProgress`).
